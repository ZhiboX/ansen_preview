02THE COLLABORATION

What is Care
Economy CRC?

Care Economy CRC (Cooperative Research Centre) brings care providers, technology businesses, researchers and government together to improve care in Australia. Its work spans technology, data and workforce innovation, with solutions shaped around the people who receive and deliver care.

Explore Care Economy CRC

Official website · Opens in a new tab

As part of Care Economy CRC, Ansen focuses on strengthening and increasing the care sector’s use of AI.

We welcome care organisations interested in discussing funded development opportunities for AI-powered tools. A conversation helps us understand your needs and explore how a potential project could fit the initiative.

Funding scope, availability and participation arrangements are discussed for each potential project.
