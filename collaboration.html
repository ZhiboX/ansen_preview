What is Care
Economy CRC?

Care Economy CRC (Cooperative Research Centre) connects care providers, researchers, technology businesses and government to improve care through technology, data and workforce innovation.

Explore Care Economy CRC

Ansen’s role

We help care organisations explore AI, develop software and plan how new tools fit into everyday work.

As part of Care Economy CRC, we welcome conversations about funded AI development. Funding availability, project scope and participation are agreed for each project.
